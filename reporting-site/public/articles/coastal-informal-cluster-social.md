# Social copy

**LinkedIn**

What changes when a “coastal risk” ranking finally uses spatial data?

Across 1,334 reporting urban centres in 24 ADB developing economies, the
population recorded below 10 metres grew by 90.9 million from 2000 to 2020.
Shanghai, Bangkok, and Dhaka lead. The top ten centres account for 52.1% of
positive change.

Only two of the inherited proxy's top five economies remain. The lesson is
methodological: a national population-and-slum score cannot substitute for a
city-level elevation object.

This is exposure growth—not flood loss, informality, or policy performance.
The next research step is to join city hazard, subsidence, protection, and
