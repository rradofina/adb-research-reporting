# What this result does not mean

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

CPPI is an observed vessel-time measure, not a complete logistics-chain
measure. It ends at the port boundary and does not observe inland destination,
customs release, corridor reliability, road or rail impedance, cost, or firm-
level logistics outcomes.

The economy summaries are deliberately simple diagnostics created for this
construct test. They are not official CPPI country rankings, do not identify
the best or worst logistics system, and do not justify resource allocation.
Coverage differs across years and ports; country medians can conceal large
within-economy dispersion.

The inherited score is also not useless in every role. Import scale remains an
exposure quantity, and LPI remains a perception-based national logistics
indicator. The rejected step is labeling their product as observed port or
hinterland friction.

Finally, the LPI 2.0 interactive shipment file is behind a Cloudflare challenge
in this environment. The study records that source boundary rather than
substituting another proxy. Human-final review and any owner-mediated source
access remain outside the AI-first chain.
