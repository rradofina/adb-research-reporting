# Conclusion and next evidence upgrade

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

The original topic contained two different ideas: dependence on a narrow generation mix and heat-related electricity interruptions. Public data support the first as a structural screen. They do not support the second as a regional direction or ranking.

The next claim-enabling object is not another country proxy. It is a service-territory or event panel with interruption start and end times, customers or load affected, daily or hourly heat, demand, available generation, imports, maintenance, and network conditions. A credible design would exploit within-territory variation and pre-specify heat thresholds and lag windows.

Until that object is available, the publishable finding is the measurement mismatch itself: a plausible mechanism does not become a regional empirical result merely because national datasets can be joined.
