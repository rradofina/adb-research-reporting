# What we found

An absolute emigrant-stock ranking is useful when the question is the scale of a diaspora or service population abroad. It cannot, by itself, show how large that stock is relative to the population remaining at origin. Migration theory also cautions against reading a cumulative stock as a current propensity or flow [@dehaas2021aspirations].

UN DESA 2024 emigrant stock is available for all 44 economies in the program panel. A World Bank WDI 2024 resident-population denominator is available for 41. The comparison withholds Taipei,China, Cook Islands, and Niue rather than fabricating values.

| Measure | Leading five origins |
|---|---|
| Absolute emigrant stock | India, China, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, Philippines |
| Stock relative to resident population | Samoa, Tonga, Armenia, Nauru, Fiji |

The two sets have **zero overlap**. The result is unchanged at the top three; at the top eight, only Afghanistan appears in both sets.

![Paired rank lists show zero overlap between the absolute and population-share top fives.](/programs/migration-displacement-signals/generated/charts/migration-rank-inversion.svg)

Samoa's emigrant stock is 54.73% of its resident population and Tonga's is 51.10%. Armenia, Nauru, and Fiji range from 19.49% to 21.02%. These are stock-to-population ratios, not estimates of the share of residents who migrated in 2024 and not forecasts of future movement.

![Bars show the leading population-normalized origin stocks and the much lower shares for India and China.](/programs/migration-displacement-signals/generated/charts/migration-population-share-profile.svg)

The population-share top five are not forced-displacement-majority origins in the UNHCR crosswalk. Afghanistan is the near-rank exception: it is sixth on the population-share measure, and UNHCR forced-displacement stock equals 81.7% of its UN DESA emigrant stock. That threshold result holds at 25%, 50%, and 75%.

![Composition bars distinguish Afghanistan's forced-displacement-majority stock from the population-share top five.](/programs/migration-displacement-signals/generated/charts/migration-forced-displacement-composition.svg)

# What this means

Use absolute stock for questions about scale; use the population denominator when the question is stock relative to origin population; and examine UNHCR categories before interpreting displacement. Purpose-specific policy analysis requires public flow, visa, recruitment, return, or household evidence beyond this stock panel.

# What this does not say

Do not infer current flows, migration propensity, labor-migration purpose, internal displacement, welfare, or causal drivers.
