# Upgrade gap — Migration and displacement signals

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

The current issue is complete as a stock-denominator and source-construct
audit. A stronger issue requires a different public data object.

## Highest-value next object

A comparable origin-destination **flow by migration purpose and year** for a
small, policy-relevant set of Pacific, Caucasus, South Asian, and Afghanistan
corridors. Candidate public layers include national deployment registers,
visa-class statistics, student-mobility tables, return records, UN DESA flow
series where definitions are compatible, and IDMC internal-displacement data.

## What the upgrade would answer

- whether Samoa and Tonga's high stock shares reflect active flows or older
  diaspora accumulation;
- how much of the residual stock is labor, family, student, or temporary
  mobility;
- whether corridor concentration persists in annual flows; and
- how international and internal forced movement differ for Afghanistan and
  other affected origins.

## Entry condition

Do not reopen the issue with another stock ranking. Begin only when at least
one eligible flow or migration-purpose object has public documentation,
stable geography codes, a retrieval record, and enough common years or
corridors to support a pre-specified comparison.
