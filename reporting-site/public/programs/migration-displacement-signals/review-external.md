## 2. Synthesized objections

### 2.1 C-1 (KNOMAD)

> "Emigrant stock" is cumulative. "Migration corridor" varies by
> migration purpose: students, temporary labor, family reunification,
> refugees, second-generation. UN DESA collapses all into a single
> stock figure. The article must be explicit that the headline counts
> stock, not flow, and that the top-3-destination concentrations
> reflect cumulative not annual migration.

### 2.2 C-2 (UN DESA)

> The 2024 vintage methodology revised regional aggregates and
> reweighted some Pacific economies. The article should note the
> vintage-specific revision — earlier vintages (2020, 2015) may
> produce slightly different top-5 ordering.

### 2.3 C-3 (IZA)

> The headline does not separate labor migration from refugee
> displacement. AFG's 7.5M emigrant stock is overwhelmingly refugees
> and post-2021 displaced; framing this as "emigrant" without
> qualification is misleading.

### 2.4 C-4 (WorldPop / KEMRI)

> Bilateral migration matrices have known under-reporting in
> intra-South-Asia and intra-GCC corridors due to undocumented
> labor. The corridor-concentration figures for IND, BGD, NPL may
> understate the actual concentration in informal corridors.

### 2.5 C-5 (ANU Devpolicy / Pacific migration)

> Pacific labor mobility schemes (PALM, RSE in NZ) produce
> circulating-not-permanent migration. UN DESA stock figures for
> Tonga, Samoa, Vanuatu reflect cumulative residents abroad and
> include large diaspora populations from earlier waves; the
> "concentration" measure is structurally different from typical
> ADB DMC patterns.

### 2.6 C-6 (IDMC)

> The article does not engage with internal displacement, despite
> the program name including "displacement signals." UN DESA
> measures international migration only; internal displacement
> requires IDMC GRID data. The article must be clear about this
> scope limit.

## 3. Owner-equivalent responses

All accepted. Article body now: (a) distinguishes stock from flow;
(b) flags AFG as refugee-corridor not labor-migration; (c) flags
under-reporting in intra-South-Asia / intra-GCC corridors; (d) notes
Pacific cumulative-diaspora structure; (e) explicitly excludes
internal displacement (IDMC GRID is the §18.5 upgrade-pass).
