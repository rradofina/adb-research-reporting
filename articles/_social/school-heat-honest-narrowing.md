# Short post

> Cambodia ranked first in our inherited school-heat proxy—but sixth of six by
> affected-student count among ADB economies where UNICEF identified heatwave
> as the major 2024 disruption hazard. The proxy's rank correlation is +0.03;
> child population alone is +0.94. We retire the ranking.

# Expanded post

> The old story also said Cambodia remained first in every ±50% perturbation.
> The committed runs show five of six discriminating wins, one Pakistan lead,
> and one all-zero tie.
>
> UNICEF's annex provides the first direct outcome check: 21 ADB rows, six
> heatwave-major cases, and 154.9 million affected students. The sample is
> small and selected, counts are not days or learning loss, and missing rows
> are unknown—not zero.
>
> This is not a replacement country ranking. The next study must align local
> daily heat, school calendars, enrolled students, and an observed closure,
> attendance, or learning outcome.
