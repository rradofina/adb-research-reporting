# The finding

Five coastal ADB DMCs hold the top of the urban-informal-pressure
ranking when scaled by population: Pakistan, Philippines, China,
Bangladesh, Myanmar. Stable across +/- 50 percent on the slum-
imputation value (5%, 10%, 15%).

The index is intentionally simple: log10(population) × urban-share
× slum-share, restricted to the 31 ADB DMCs with a coastline and
WDI inputs.

# What it cannot say

- WDI's slum series is sparse — only 7 of 31 coastal DMCs have direct
  data. The other 24 are imputed at 10%. The headline survives
  ±50% on this imputation value but a real slum-share series for
  every DMC is the upgrade-pass.
- The "coastal" flag is binary. LECZ (Low-Elevation Coastal Zone)
  population-share is the standard exposure measure and is the
  §18.5 upgrade-pass.
- The log-population term down-weights Pacific small-island states
  whose entire populations are coastal-LECZ. Their per-capita
  vulnerability is hidden by the absolute-scale headline.
- Coastal exposure ≠ coastal-informal risk. Cyclone, storm-surge,
  and sea-level-rise scenarios are the actionable layers; this
  artifact is structural-exposure only.
