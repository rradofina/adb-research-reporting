# Results — construct validation

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · PP

## Main result

The published set `{BGD, LAO, MMR, PAK, PHL}` overlaps the panel's actual
value-ranked set `{PAK, VUT, MMR, LAO, TJK}` in three of five places. Vanuatu
(13.6) and Tajikistan (3.7) outrank the Philippines (2.8) and Bangladesh (2.7)
but were excluded because each has one missing proxy leg.

Mean imputation preserves the substitution: Vanuatu and Tajikistan remain in;
the Philippines and Bangladesh remain out.

## Coverage-construct result

The current all-SP and safety-net variants produce `{PNG, SLB, TLS, FSM, TKM}`,
zero overlap with the published set. Their inputs span 1998–2022 and are too
old and incomplete to support a replacement ranking.

## Direct response result

The World Bank COVID-19 matrix documents a cash-based transfer response in all
five published economies. Across 24 economies with a computed gap and response
row, the gap's Spearman association with eight-category response breadth is
−0.07 (95% bootstrap interval −0.47 to 0.36). The source has no comparable
successful-receipt, delivery-time, payment-failure, or trigger-latency outcome.

## Decision

Reject the readiness ranking. Preserve the source and method audit as the
research finding.
