# 1 · Finding

**+90.9 million people** recorded below 10 metres across 1,334 reporting urban
centres, 2000–2020.

![Growth hero](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-01-growth-hero.png){width=88%}

# 2 · Named centres drive the aggregate

Shanghai, Bangkok, and Dhaka lead. The top ten centres account for 52.1% of
positive change.

![Concentration](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-10-growth-concentration.png){width=88%}

# 3 · The old ranking was a different construct

Only China and Bangladesh appear in both top-five economy sets.

![Proxy comparison](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-03-proxy-falsification.png){width=88%}

# 4 · A second quantity corroborates the pattern

Population and built-up change below 10 metres correlate at 0.85.

![Population and built-up](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-07-population-built-corroboration.png){width=88%}

# 5 · Shares can conceal counts

255 centres gained low-elevation population while the share fell.

![Absolute and proportional change](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-08-absolute-versus-share.png){width=88%}

# 6 · Elevation sensitivity

The 2000–2020 increase is +34.6 million below 5 metres and +90.9 million below
10 metres.

![Elevation sensitivity](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-05-elevation-sensitivity.png){width=88%}

# 7 · ±50% time-window sensitivity

Every 10-, 20-, and 30-year run is positive under both thresholds.

![Window sensitivity](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-06-window-sensitivity.png){width=88%}

# 8 · Coverage is the binding limit

The source-matched roster has 5,347 centres; 1,334 report the required block.
No blank is converted to zero.

![Coverage funnel](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-11-coverage-funnel.png){width=88%}

# 9 · Claim gate

The object measures exposure growth. It does not identify flood loss,
informality, deprivation, protection, or policy quality.

![Claim gate](/programs/coastal-informal-risk/generated/charts/coastal-informal-risk-12-method-and-claim-gate.png){width=88%}
