# Climate-health labor-capacity construct validation

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · maturity PP · rebuilt 2026-07-18

## Finding

Across 21 aligned year-and-parameter tests, the inherited WDI PM2.5 ×
employment proxy shares at most one of its top three economies with the Lancet
Countdown 2025 indicator 1.1.3 estimate of heat-related potential work hours
lost. Sixteen tests have zero overlap and five have one.

The program therefore retires the old “workday-loss pressure” ranking. It
publishes a construct-validation result: the PM2.5 proxy and the heat-capacity
measure are different evidence objects.

## What is now available

- a 44-economy 2024 heat-labor panel with source and unit records;
- aligned 2018–2020 proxy-versus-heat comparisons for 34 economies;
- 21 baseline and ±50% parameter tests;
- seven evidence figures plus a research hero;
- a full paper, brief, blog, social card, and slide source;
- reproduction instructions, review records, and a public evidence route.

## Non-claims

Potential work hours lost are modelled capacity losses, not recorded absence,
hours worked, output, income, or a causal estimate from this study. The analysis
does not combine heat and PM2.5 into a composite and does not rank economy
performance or policy quality.
