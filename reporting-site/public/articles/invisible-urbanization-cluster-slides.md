# 1 · Finding

**20.0 percentage points**

Median absolute GHSL–WDI urban-share gap across 40 complete ADB developing-
economy cases, 2020.

![Definition-gap hero](/programs/invisible-urbanization/generated/charts/invisible-urbanization-01-definition-gap-hero.png){width=88%}

# 2 · Two valid but different constructs

- WDI: urban population under national definitions.
- GHSL: one harmonized grid-based Degree of Urbanisation method.
- The gap is a construct disagreement, not an error estimate.

![Method and claim gate](/programs/invisible-urbanization/generated/charts/invisible-urbanization-11-method-and-claim-gate.png){width=88%}

# 3 · Both directions

GHSL is higher in 33 cases; WDI is higher in seven.

![Selected contrasts](/programs/invisible-urbanization/generated/charts/invisible-urbanization-02-selected-definition-dumbbell.png){width=88%}

# 4 · Persistent difference

The median absolute gap stays near 20–26 points across 1975–2020.

![Definition gap over time](/programs/invisible-urbanization/generated/charts/invisible-urbanization-03-definition-gap-over-time.png){width=88%}

# 5 · Administrative scale changes the answer

Same 13 economies: 0.6% embedded at level 1, 1.9% at level 2, 2.8% at level 3.

![Scale sensitivity](/programs/invisible-urbanization/generated/charts/invisible-urbanization-05-administrative-scale-sensitivity.png){width=88%}

# 6 · The stock fell

Across the same 34 level-2-covered economies, the embedded share fell from
7.4% in 1975 to 2.0% in 2020.

![Embedded share](/programs/invisible-urbanization/generated/charts/invisible-urbanization-06-embedded-share-over-time.png){width=88%}

# 7 · But persistent rural units kept growing

From 2000–2020, units that stayed rural gained 13.9 million urban-cell
residents. Rural-to-town/city threshold crossings removed 43.3 million from the
embedded category.

![Transition decomposition](/programs/invisible-urbanization/generated/charts/invisible-urbanization-07-transition-waterfall.png){width=88%}

# 8 · Use and limit

Use national definitions for national reporting and GHSL for harmonized
comparison. Show both when they diverge.

Do not infer legal misclassification, neglect, services, or welfare from the
gap alone.

# 9 · Next study

GHSL grids, and one service or fiscal outcome.
