## Objections

**C-1 (WRI Aqueduct).** Aqueduct uses baseline water stress (BWS),
projected stress, drought risk separately. WDI ER.H2O.FWTL.ZS is a
single point-in-time measure; doesn't capture trajectory.

**C-2 (IWMI/CGIAR).** Withdrawal-share-of-resources can exceed 100%
when supplemented by transboundary or fossil-aquifer water. TKM's
1,868% is largely Amu Darya inflow; treating it as "stress" within
TKM borders is misleading.

**C-3 (FAO AQUASTAT).** AQUASTAT is the canonical source; WDI is
derived. Direct AQUASTAT pull would have additional series
(per-sector withdrawal: agri vs industrial vs municipal) for richer
analysis.

**C-4 (Central Asia).** Aral Basin (UZB, KGZ, TJK, TKM, KAZ) needs
transboundary-cooperation framing. Single-country pressure index
hides the inter-state mechanism.

## Responses

All accepted. Article: (a) frames TKM as transboundary-dependent;
(b) §18.5 upgrade-pass uses Aqueduct BWS + projected stress; (c) FAO
AQUASTAT direct pull deferred; (d) Central Asia transboundary
context documented.
