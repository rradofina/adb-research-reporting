# Retired result — the national flood-rural proxy was not market access

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · Decision recorded 2026-07-19

The former program headline ranked ADB developing member economies using:

`rural share × annual EM-DAT flood-event count × log10(population)`.

India, China, Indonesia, and Afghanistan remained in the top four under several
metric formulations. That stability did not validate the label “market access.”
The index contained no road graph, flood footprint, market destination, trip,
or access outcome. It was a composite triage screen and is retired as a research
finding.

The negative result mattered because it changed the research question. Instead
of asking which countries score high on a broad proxy, the program asked whether
one public, date-aligned flood × road × market × population object could be
built. The Sylhet pilot answers that narrower question and exposes the remaining
passability and destination gates.

The old generated panel remains in the repository for traceability. It must not
be presented as corroboration for the Sylhet route estimate or as a national
ranking of flood-induced market isolation.
