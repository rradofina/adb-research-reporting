# Deepened result — from national proxy to routed event object

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

## Decision

The access claim changed shape. The national composite ranking is retired, and
the program now reports one bounded, event-specific route result.

## Why the new object is stronger

The old index could only say that some large or rural economies recorded many
threshold-crossing flood events. It could not identify a flooded road, a market,
an origin, or a route. The Sylhet object joins all four and calculates a
population-weighted change in reachability.

## Result

Under the base mechanical road cut, 345,718 modeled people—41.24% of those with
a baseline route—lose access to the eight mapped destinations. The range remains
38.92%–43.45% across 54 variants.

## Stronger boundary found during presentation

The first map exposed query-box roads and markets outside the UNOSAT polygon.
Restricting both to the observed footprint changed the estimate by only 0.02
percentage points. The presentation pass therefore improved the empirical
object, not only its appearance.

## Remaining wall

The next step is outcome validation: depth- or field-calibrated passability and
an audited market inventory. A third proxy or source-readiness pass would not
move this claim.
