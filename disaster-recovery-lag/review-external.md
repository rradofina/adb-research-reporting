## Synthesized objections

**C-1 (CRED).** EM-DAT thresholds (≥10 deaths or ≥100 affected or
declared emergency or international aid) systematically miss small-
scale repeated events. The article must note that EM-DAT undercounts
the long-tail of small disasters.

**C-2 (UNDRR).** Sendai Framework disaster-loss accounting is the
standard. Comparing absolute burden across DMCs without normalization
violates the Sendai per-capita convention. Article must report
per-capita figures alongside absolutes.

**C-3 (WB GFDRR).** Damage-USD CPI-adjustment alone does not capture
purchasing-power-parity differences. PPP-adjusted damage gives a
different ranking.

**C-4 (Pacific climate-adaptation).** The dataset misses non-event
slow-onset climate stress (sea-level encroachment, salinization,
recurrent king tides). For Pacific DMCs, EM-DAT-only is structurally
incomplete.

## Responses

All accepted. Article body adds (a) per-capita CHN and IND figures
alongside absolutes, (b) explicit EM-DAT under-reporting caveat for
small DMCs, (c) note that Pacific slow-onset stress is out-of-scope.
