# Upgrade gap — Climate-health labor capacity

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

The current issue is complete as a construct-validation and denominator-repair
study. A stronger issue requires observed labor outcomes.

## Highest-value next object

A public subnational or workplace panel that can join heat exposure to observed
absenteeism, hours worked, physical output, or labor supply for a small set of
economies. The object must document workplace location, time, sector, worker
coverage, adaptation, and outcome definition.

## What the upgrade would answer

- whether modelled potential capacity losses predict actual work behavior;
- how cooling, shade, shift timing, rest breaks, and mechanization alter the
  exposure-outcome relationship;
- whether national sector-share assumptions conceal within-economy variation;
  and
- whether PM2.5 and heat have separable or interacting effects in an identified
  design.

## Entry condition

Do not reopen with another composite or national ranking. Begin only when a
public observed-outcome object has stable geography and time identifiers,
documented coverage, a permissible license, and enough overlap with exposure
data to freeze a falsifiable analysis before estimation.
