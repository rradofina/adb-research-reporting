# Paper charter — low-elevation urban growth

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

## Core question

Which urban centres in ADB developing economies recorded the largest increase
in population and built-up surface below 5 and 10 metres from 2000 to 2020?

## Core claim

The reporting subset added 90.9 million people below 10 metres; growth is
concentrated in named centres and changes the inherited national ranking.

## Contribution

A direct, reproducible settlement-scale exposure object plus an honest
falsification of an imputation-heavy country proxy.

## Nonclaims

No flood probability, expected loss, informality, deprivation, protection,
adaptation, or policy-performance inference.

## Decision user

Researchers choosing which cities merit local hazard, protection, and service-
data deepening; policy teams interpreting coastal-exposure counts and shares.

## Stopping rule

Publish at PP when source custody, protocol deviation, figure dossier,
sensitivity, literature position, review synthesis, and public tiers pass the
repository gates. Do not add a third generic data audit in place of a claim.
