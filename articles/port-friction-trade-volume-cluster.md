# The finding

| ISO3 | LPI | Index |
|---|---|---|
| CHN | 3.7 | 1.45 |
| IND | 3.4 | 0.94 |
| IDN | 3.1 | 0.66 |
| VNM | 3.3 | 0.63 |
| THA | 3.5 | 0.54 |

Stable across +/- 50 percent perturbation of the imports-volume
normalizer (50 → 25 / 75) and the imports cap (2.0 → 1.0 / 3.0).

# What it cannot say

- LPI is a **perception-based survey**, not a measured throughput
  figure. The "logistics gap" is a logistics-perception gap.
- The friction is **country-aggregate**, not per-corridor. Bilateral
  trade-cost data (OECD) would be more actionable.
- **Landlocked DMCs** (LAO, MNG, central Asia) have a structurally
  different friction story — transit-country dependence — and are
  aggregated here with coastal economies.
- **Throughput / vessel-tracking (AIS)** would provide direct port-
  friction measures but is licensable.
