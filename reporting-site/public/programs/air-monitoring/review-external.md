## Objections

**C-1 (OpenAQ).** Monitor counts include reference-grade and low-
cost sensors heterogeneously. The article should distinguish.

**C-2 (WHO).** The 5 µg/m³ guideline (2021) is the most stringent
ambient annual mean recommendation. The interim targets (35, 25,
15, 10, 5) are policy stepping-stones. Using the 5 µg/m³ as the
single threshold is correct but should be paired with interim-
target reporting for policy actionability.

**C-3 (Dalhousie ACAG).** WDI's national PM2.5 is monitor-
interpolated; in low-monitor DMCs, it's effectively imputed.
ACAG-V6 satellite-derived 1-km gridded PM2.5 fills this and is
the upgrade-pass.

**C-4 (HEI).** Monitor density correlates with HDI globally. The
gap-score's high values (AFG, MMR) reflect both high pollution
AND low monitoring infrastructure; these are not independent
phenomena.

**C-5 (WB Air-Quality).** Actionable policy needs source-
attribution (transport vs household biomass vs industrial vs
agricultural burning). The observability-gap doesn't address
attribution.

## Responses

All accepted. Article: (a) flags reference-grade vs low-cost
heterogeneity; (b) reports interim targets alongside 5 µg/m³;
(c) ACAG-V6 deferred to §18.5; (d) HDI-correlation acknowledged in
limitations; (e) source attribution out-of-scope.
