# The finding

EM-DAT 2000–2025 records disaster burden for 38 of 50 ADB regional
DMCs. Across three different burden metrics, the top-2 is identical:
**China** and **India** in some order, by every measure.

| Metric | #1 | #2 |
|---|---|---|
| Events per year | CHN (25.6) | IDN (15.7) — IND third (15.5) |
| Total affected (people) | CHN (1.77 B) | IND (1.15 B) |
| Total damage USD-adj | CHN | IND |

The top-3 contains IDN and PHL by event-frequency but those positions
do not survive the affected-count or damage-USD ranking. **The
metric-robust top-2 is the headline.**

The two economies dominate by absolute scale. Per-capita normalization
shifts the picture entirely toward Pacific small-island vulnerability
and is a separate question (§18.5 upgrade-pass).

# What it cannot say

- **Burden ≠ recovery lag.** The program name implies a recovery-lag
  question (post-event indicator-recovery curves); the current
  artifact reports burden only. Recovery analysis requires per-event
  timestamped indicator data joined to EM-DAT events.
- **EM-DAT thresholds undercount small DMCs.** The ≥10-deaths-or-
  ≥100-affected entry threshold misses long-tail small-DMC events.
- **PPP-adjustment not applied.** Damage-USD CPI-adjusted; PPP gives
  a different ranking.
- **Pacific slow-onset stress excluded.** Sea-level encroachment,
  salinization, and king tides do not produce single-event entries.
