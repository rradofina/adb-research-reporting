# Negative result — the inherited index did not measure coastal risk

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

The previous index multiplied national population, urban share, and slum share,
then restricted the roster to economies manually identified as coastal. Most
slum shares were imputed at a common 10% value. Its ±50% sensitivity changed
that common imputation to 5% and 15%.

That test could not validate the ranking: multiplying the same rows by the same
positive factor preserves their order. More importantly, the formula contained
no coastline, elevation, settlement footprint, flood surface, or protection
measure. It ranked national scale under a coastal label.

The direct GHS-UCDB object changes three of the top five economies. This is not
a parameter adjustment; it is a construct falsification. The old index is
retained only as a documented negative result and is not used on the public
research surface.
