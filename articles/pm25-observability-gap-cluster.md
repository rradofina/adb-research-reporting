# The finding

Five economies hold the top-5 PM2.5 observability-gap positions.

| ISO3 | PM2.5 µg/m³ | Public monitors | Gap score |
|---|---|---|---|
| AFG | 46.1 | 2 | 100 |
| BGD | high | low | high |
| MMR | high | low | high |
| UZB | high | low | high |
| TJK | high | low | high |

# What it cannot say

- Subnational exposure — WDI is country-mean.
- Source attribution — transport / household / industrial / burning.
- Reference-grade vs low-cost monitor distinction.
- HDI correlation: high gap-score reflects both high pollution AND
  low monitoring infrastructure; these are not independent.
