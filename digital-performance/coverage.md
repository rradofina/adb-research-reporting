# Data sources and coverage

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · 2026-07-19

## Source custody

All analysis inputs come from the public ITU DataHub v2 API. The committed
inventory records nine response objects, including indicator metadata, annual
series, retrieval timestamps, response sizes, and SHA-256 digests. Raw JSON is
kept in the ignored program cache and is regenerated by the acquisition script.

## Headline exclusions

The 2024 cross-section has no exact-year pair for Cook Islands, Marshall
Islands, Myanmar, Niue, Palau, Samoa, Timor-Leste, Turkmenistan, Tuvalu, and
Vanuatu. These rows are missing, not zero.

## Indicator definitions

- **4G/LTE population coverage (`i271GA`):** people within range of at least a
  4G/LTE mobile-cellular signal, whether or not they subscribe or use it.
- **Individuals using the Internet (`i99H`):** people who used the Internet
  from any location in the previous three months, by fixed or mobile network.
- **5 GB price (`i271mb_5GB_GNI`):** the data-only mobile-broadband 5 GB basket
  as a percentage of GNI per capita.
- **Urban/rural use:** location-disaggregated internet-use rates. The script
  subtracts rural from urban only on exact economy-year matches.

## Provenance and consistency checks

Six of the 34 headline pairs identify both component values as ITU estimates;
the remaining 28 include at least one national or other named source. The
returned source strings and notes remain in the CSV. One 2019 Indonesia row has
reported 4G coverage 0.65 points above 3G coverage. It is retained and flagged
rather than silently repaired.

## License and access

The ITU DataHub is accessible through public HTTPS without authentication. The
analysis republishes small derived tables and charts under the repository's
research-output license; it does not ship the raw API mirror with the site.
