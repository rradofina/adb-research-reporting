# Decision in one line

Keep fuel concentration as a structural-exposure screen, but do not publish a regional heat-vulnerability ranking: 15 exact-year heat–reliability correlations split eight positive to seven negative, and 10 uncertainty intervals include zero.

## What survives

- The same five economies top fuel concentration on installed capacity and 2017 generation.
- Generation is usually more concentrated, showing that built backup can overstate diversity.
- A public exact-year World Bank CCKP × reliability-proxy bridge exists for 36 economies.

## What fails

The direction changes with the heat measure, reliability outcome, and country weighting. Fuel concentration also has no stable latest-per-economy relationship with the five reliability proxies.

## Next data object

Use service-territory interruption records joined to hourly or daily heat, demand, available generation, imports, maintenance, and network conditions. No third national-proxy pass.
