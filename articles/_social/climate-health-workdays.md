# Short post

> A stable proxy can still measure the wrong thing. Across 21 aligned tests,
> our PM2.5 × employment screen shares at most 1 of 3 leading economies with
> the Lancet heat-work-loss measure. Potential hours are modelled—not recorded
> absence.

# Expanded post

> The old climate-health screen kept naming India, Afghanistan, and Bangladesh
> when its settings changed. But an external construct test changed the story.
>
> In aligned 2020 data, the Lancet Countdown heat-related potential work-hours-
> loss top three are Cambodia, Myanmar, and Thailand: zero overlap. Across 21
> year × ±50% parameter tests, 16 have zero overlap and five have one.
>
> The conclusion is not that PM2.5 has no labor effect. Heat and pollution are
> different pathways. The proxy is retired as a heat-loss substitute, and the
> next study must join observed absence, hours, or output.
