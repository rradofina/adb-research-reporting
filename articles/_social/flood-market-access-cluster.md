# LinkedIn

A stable proxy is still a proxy.

We replaced a national flood × rural-population ranking with an event-level
route object for Sylhet: a UNOSAT observed flood footprint, historical OSM roads
and marketplaces, and WorldPop population.

Under the base stress test, 345,718 modeled people—41.24% of those with a
baseline route—lose access to eight mapped marketplaces. The result remains
38.92%–43.45% across 54 variants.

The map also caught a spatial eligibility bug before publication. After roads
and markets outside the observed footprint were removed, the finding survived.

Important limit: water intersection is not observed road closure, and eight OSM
markets are not an audited destination census. This is a routed-access stress
test, not an impact or welfare estimate.
