# Paper charter

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

A grid that runs on one fuel looks fragile on paper, and heat stress is a
plausible reason outages rise in summer. Those are two different questions.
Public national tables support a stable fuel-concentration screen; they do
not support a stable heat-to-reliability ranking across measures.

- **Primary finding:** Capacity-to-generation fuel concentration is stable in membership; heat-to-reliability direction is not stable across measures.
- **Decision:** Replace the heat-reliability ranking with a two-gate construct-validation story.
- **Contribution:** A transparent exact-year public-data bridge that shows where regional comparability ends.
- **Non-claims:** No causal effect, vulnerability ranking, outage prediction, adequacy assessment, or current plant-mix claim.
- **Reader value:** Prevents a structurally plausible hypothesis from becoming an unsupported regional ranking and specifies the data object needed next.
- **Stop rule:** Do not take a third proxy pass. The next pass must obtain event-level or service-territory reliability data or rotate.
