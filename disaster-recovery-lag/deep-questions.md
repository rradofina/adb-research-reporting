`attestation_chain: ai-first`

## Resolved in this issue

1. **Is the inherited burden top two stable?** No. Three of five metrics change
   at least one member.
2. **Is a public event-to-radiance join technically possible?** Yes. The World
   Bank archive supports credential-free fixed-orbit extraction.
3. **Does the Haiyan pilot yield a stable recovery month?** No. Zero of seven
   centroids retain one month across 54 variants.
4. **Is missingness negligible?** No. Only one centroid has four valid
   pre-event main-specification months.
5. **Can GDIS centroids be accepted without screening?** No. Three gross
   country mismatches are detected, and even correct centroids remain
   administrative approximations.

## Open only for a new evidence object

1. Do verified hazard or damage footprints materially reduce specification
   sensitivity relative to administrative-centroid windows?
2. Does settlement-weighted radiance agree with electricity-restoration,
   facility-operation, school-reopening, firm, or household measures?
3. Can a longer and denser baseline distinguish sensor volatility from local
   disruption?
4. Which comparison-area design is credible for a given hazard and footprint?
5. Does an event-specific validated design transport across hazards, settlement
   brightness, cloud regimes, and power systems?

These questions do not authorize another centroid-threshold sweep. Reopening
requires a named footprint or independent outcome that can change the claim.
