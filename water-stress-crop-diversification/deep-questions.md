# Deep questions — after the national ranking failed

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

This is a research agenda, not a set of findings. The country ranking has been
retired; the next questions start from public data objects and shared units.

## 1. Basin allocation

For the Amu Darya, Syr Darya, Indus, Helmand, and other relevant basins, what
share of annual and seasonal renewable flow is withdrawn, consumed, returned,
or secured by allocation? A national ratio cannot distinguish upstream control
from downstream use.

**Needed object:** basin-year withdrawal, renewable flow, environmental flow,
allocation, and return-flow ledger.

## 2. Crop location and irrigation status

Which harvested areas are inside stressed basins, and which are irrigated
rather than rain-fed? National FAOSTAT shares do not place crops in water units.

**Needed object:** SPAM or another public crop map, irrigation mask, basin
geometry, crop calendar, and common reference year.

## 3. Crop water demand

Do concentrated crop systems also concentrate consumptive water demand during
the basin's scarce months?

**Needed object:** crop coefficients, evapotranspiration, effective rainfall,
irrigation requirement, and harvested area by crop-month-basin.

## 4. Observed outcome

Does crop diversity moderate production loss, yield variance, farm-income loss,
or recovery after a drought or depletion shock?

**Needed object:** a panel of crop or farm outcomes linked to drought/depletion
events, with a declared comparison design. This is the test required by the
diversification literature.

## 5. Missing small-island water data

Can public national or geospatial sources provide a comparable freshwater
availability object for Tuvalu, Kiribati, the Federated States of Micronesia,
Nauru, and Vanuatu? The current direct crop leaders are all absent from SDG
6.4.2.

**Stop condition:** if no comparable withdrawal-based indicator is meaningful
for small island systems, publish that measurement mismatch and use a separate
rainfall, storage, desalination, or groundwater-lens construct rather than
forcing them into the continental metric.

## Keystone

Build one pilot basin × crop × irrigation × year join before adding another
country. If the pilot cannot align water, crop demand, and an observed outcome,
stop at the construct-validation paper rather than expanding the roster.
