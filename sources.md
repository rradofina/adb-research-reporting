# Sources — Living Authoritative List

This is the live, working list of sources that every program's `literature.md`
must search. It is governed by `CONSTITUTION.md` §4.2. Updates to this file do
not require a constitutional amendment; they require a commit with the
reasoning in the message.

**Floor vs. ceiling.** Tiers A and B are mandatory for every program. Tier C
is mandatory where the topic applies. Tier D is mandatory for every DMC in
scope. Tier E is used for completeness verification. Programs extend with
topic-specific sources in their own `literature.md`.

Last updated: 2026-04-24.

## Tier D — Country and national-agency sources (check for every DMC in scope)

For each DMC in scope, search:

- National statistical office (PSA for PHL, BBS for BGD, PBS for PAK, NSO
  NP for NPL, DCS for LKA, NIS for KHM, LSB for LAO, DGE for TLS, BPS for
  IDN, GSO for VNM, etc.).
- Central bank research department (BSP, Bangladesh Bank, SBP, NRB, CBSL,
  NBC, BOL, BCTL, BI, SBV, BoT, NBK, etc.).
- National planning agency (NEDA, Planning Commission, Bappenas, MPI, etc.).
- Country open-data portal and national SDG reporting platform.
- Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX) country dataset listings.
