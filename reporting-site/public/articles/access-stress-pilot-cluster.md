# The finding

8-DMC pilot. 104 ADM1 units. Country-level access-stress aggregated
two ways. Common top-4: **{BGD, KHM, LAO, PAK}** — stable.

# What it cannot say

- Travel-time access (the actionable measure) — facility-count proxy
  only.
- Coverage beyond 8 pilot DMCs — 42 remaining DMCs are out-of-scope.
- Sectoral disaggregation — health/edu/market lumped.
- OSM under-counts the official registry (per PSDQ for PHL/BGD).
