# Social copy

**LinkedIn**

A reproducible logistics ranking can still measure the wrong thing.

An inherited screen multiplied import scale by a perceived national logistics
gap and labeled the result port-hinterland friction. Tested against World Bank
vessel-time data, only one economy remains in its top five. Across 20
specifications, overlap stays between zero and two.

The decision is not to publish a replacement country ranking. It is to retire
the proxy, use CPPI for the port boundary, and leave the inland corridor step
open until observed shipment evidence is available.

Measurement result—not a logistics-system quality judgment.
