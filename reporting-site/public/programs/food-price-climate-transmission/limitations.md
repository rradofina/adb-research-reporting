# Limitations — Food-price construct validation

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · 2026-07-19

The evidence supports a coincidence screen, not attribution.

1. The WFP panel covers selected markets and coarse rice, not a national basket.
2. Year-on-year comparability reduces 937 price cells to 760 analysis cells.
3. NASA POWER is monthly point rainfall at markets, not crop-zone exposure.
4. Relative dryness is not a recorded drought, flood, heat, or production shock.
5. Lags do not model crop calendars or distributed climate responses.
6. Roads, remoteness, fuel, trade, exchange rates, policy, production, and
   market integration are absent.
7. The annual CPI comparison contains five years and mismatched baskets.
8. The 81-run direction is stable, but wave and cluster counts are not.

The analysis cannot rank countries, estimate a climate effect, infer household
welfare, or generalize from coarse rice to all food prices.
