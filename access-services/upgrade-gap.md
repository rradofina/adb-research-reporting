# Upgrade gap — Access services

`attestation_chain: ai-first`. Current maturity: PP.

The current package establishes denominator sensitivity. It does not establish
service access. A human-final access study needs a different evidence object,
not more polish on the OSM ratio.

| Upgrade | Evidence needed | Decision enabled |
|---|---|---|
| Comparable facility master lists | Current, geocoded, provider-scoped registries with stable identifiers for all pilot DMCs | Separate mapping gaps from facility-distribution gaps |
| Travel-time surfaces | Routable networks, terrain, barriers, transport-mode assumptions, and population grids | Estimate geographic reach rather than point-count load |
| Facility capability | Services, staffing, operating status, beds, opening hours, and referral level | Distinguish a counted point from usable capacity |
| Demand and utilization | Household survey or administrative utilization data with ethical and disclosure review | Test whether geographic reach predicts realized use |
| Human review | Named domain, country, and statistical reviewers plus owner re-attestation | Move beyond the current §18 AI-first chain |

The immediate data engineering priority is a versioned facility crosswalk, not
an expanded composite. Until the first three upgrades are available, the
public output should remain a map-observability and source-validation study.
