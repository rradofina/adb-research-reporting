`attestation_chain: ai-first` · AI critique under Constitution §18 · 2026-07-19

## Strongest claim

The exact-year availability and use series differ materially in the 2024
observed sample. The result is readable, reproducible, and does not require the
inherited heavy speed-test route.

## Main threats

1. **Ecological subtraction:** readers may interpret the aggregate difference
   as a literal covered-but-offline population. All public copy must call it a
   measurement difference and explain why it is not a person-level partition.
2. **Current-year estimation:** ITU may revise 2024 values. The retrieval digest
   and date must stay visible.
3. **Extreme negative cases:** Kiribati's -27.4 points is a major source-
   disagreement signal. It must not be deleted or used as a substantive
   performance ranking without national metadata review.
4. **Mechanism overreach:** the weak affordability correlation does not reject
   affordability as a barrier. The strong urban–rural correlation has only ten
   cases and cannot establish a regional mechanism.
5. **Chart redundancy:** the hero, dumbbell, and scatter share the same
   cross-section but do different jobs: signed magnitude, component visibility,
   and relationship shape. Captions must maintain those distinctions.

## Changes made after critique

- Replaced “usage gap” with “availability–use measurement gap” in the primary
  claim.
- Retained negative differences and the 3G/4G hierarchy anomaly.
- Changed the affordability figure title after the observed association proved
  weak.
- Added annual sample size, a balanced time comparison, provenance groups, and
  a coverage funnel.
- Kept Ookla as a future conditional-on-test performance layer only.

## Verdict

Proceed as a Prepared Paper measurement study. Do not promote it to a causal,
household, or policy-effect claim.
