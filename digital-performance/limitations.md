# Limitations and non-claims

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · 2026-07-19

1. **The difference is not a person-level usage gap.** Coverage and use are
   separate aggregate indicators, not two outcomes observed for the same
   individuals. Subtracting them does not identify a literal count of covered
   non-users.
2. **Coverage is modeled or administratively reported availability.** It does
   not guarantee an indoor signal, capacity, reliability, latency, speed, or an
   affordable plan at a person's location.
3. **Use is not meaningful use.** Any internet use in the previous three
   months can coexist with low frequency, low data volume, poor quality, or a
   narrow range of activities.
4. **Source methods differ.** The panel combines ITU estimates, national
   administrative reports, and survey-derived values. Returned source notes
   are retained, but a full country-by-country methods audit is outside this
   paper.
5. **2024 may contain estimates.** The ITU methodology uses estimation and
   nowcasting for current-year aggregates and revises prior values as new
   national data arrive [@itu2024factsfigures]. The retrieval digest fixes this
   run, not the future DataHub vintage.
6. **Ten headline economies are missing.** The 34 observed cases cannot be
   generalized mechanically to Cook Islands, Marshall Islands, Myanmar, Niue,
   Palau, Samoa, Timor-Leste, Turkmenistan, Tuvalu, or Vanuatu.
7. **The affordability basket is national and stylized.** A 5 GB price as a
   share of GNI per capita is not household affordability, distributional
   incidence, device cost, or local market competition.
8. **The urban–rural result is small-sample.** Only ten headline cases form the
   required exact-year join. The high correlation is hypothesis-generating.
9. **No causal identification.** The analysis does not estimate the effect of
   infrastructure, price, skills, regulation, devices, content, trust, or any
   policy intervention.
10. **Ookla remains conditional on testing.** Future speed tiles can add a
    separate performance layer for places with tests. They cannot validate
    adoption or explain the primary difference.
