# Next questions — Sylhet flooded routes to market

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

1. Which water-intersecting road and bridge types were actually impassable on
   26 June 2024, and at what depth?
2. How many operating formal and informal markets existed inside and just
   outside the analysis footprint?
3. Do households and traders use the nearest mapped market, a preferred market,
   or temporary alternatives during floods?
4. How long did modeled and observed disconnection persist as water receded?
5. Which small set of road or bridge interventions restores the most validated
   population access?
6. Do observed market prices, stocks, trader arrivals, or household travel times
   change where validated access loss is largest?
7. How sensitive are results to a larger flood-observed boundary that permits
   outbound and re-entry routes?
8. Can boat, walking, and multimodal routes be represented without implying
   behavioral precision unsupported by public data?

The first two questions are claim-enabling. Until passability and destinations
are validated, further model elaboration cannot raise maturity.
