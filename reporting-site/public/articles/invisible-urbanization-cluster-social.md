# Social copy

**LinkedIn**

One country can have two very different urban shares.

Across 40 ADB developing-economy cases in 2020, the median absolute difference
between GHSL's standardized urban share and WDI's national-definition share is
20.0 percentage points.

The gap runs both ways. Administrative scale changes the answer again. And a
falling “hidden” stock can coexist with urban growth when rural units cross a
classification threshold.

The implication: show the definition, the scale, and both measures before using
an urban percentage for comparison or targeting.

This is a measurement study—not evidence of legal misclassification or service
neglect. `attestation_chain: ai-first`
