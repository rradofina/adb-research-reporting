Across all 19 overlapping UNICEF rows, including non-heat hazards, the old
index correlates +0.58 with affected count and child population +0.79. This
mixed-hazard comparison is not a heat validation.

Complete mixed-vintage enrollment denominators exist for 17 of the 19 overlap
rows. Affected-to-enrollment diagnostics are 100.1% in Bangladesh, 91.4% in
Cambodia, 89.0% in the Philippines, 48.9% in Pakistan, and 18.1% in India.
They are not comparable disruption rates.

education outcome at a matching school-day or district-day unit.
