observability finding, not a corrected country list.

## Denominator result

The baseline raw top four is Turkmenistan, Pakistan, Azerbaijan, and
Uzbekistan. Every one saturates the old water term at 1.5. Their order is
therefore determined by inverse cereal yield and rural population share.

When the direct SDG 6.4.2 denominator is used, the water-stress top five is
Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, and Tajikistan. Only Pakistan
and Turkmenistan overlap the published four.

## Crop result

The FAOSTAT harvested-area HHI top five is Tuvalu, Kiribati, the Federated
States of Micronesia, Nauru, and Vanuatu. None overlaps the published four and
none has an available-water-stress row. Coconut accounts for 71%–92% of
reported harvested area in those five records.
