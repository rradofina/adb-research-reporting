## 3. Synthesis

Two established facts:

1. **LPI is perception-based, not measured.** Surveys logistics
   experts on country-level perceptions [@wb2023lpi]. Container
   throughput and vessel tracking would be more direct
   [@unctad2024rmt].
2. **Trade volume × LPI gap = friction exposure.** Large coastal
   trade economies with mid-range LPI face the largest absolute
   logistics-performance gap.

## 4. Gap

No published cross-ADB-DMC friction-exposure ranking with ±50%
sensitivity testing on the imports-volume normalizer + cap. The
top-5 set-stability claim is the marginal contribution.

## 5. Risk of redundancy

LPI publishes country rankings biennially. The marginal contribution
is the joint product (LPI gap × trade volume) and its set stability,
not the LPI ranking itself.
