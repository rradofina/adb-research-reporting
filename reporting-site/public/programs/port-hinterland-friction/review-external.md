## Objections

**C-1 (LPI team).** LPI is a perception-based survey. A logistics-
performance gap measured by LPI is not the same as actual port-
hinterland friction (vessel-to-warehouse-to-final-mile timing).
Article must frame as a perception-of-logistics gap, not measured
friction.

**C-2 (UNCTAD).** Container-throughput data and vessel-tracking
(AIS) provide more direct port-friction measures. The §18.5
upgrade-pass should use AIS data where licensable.

**C-3 (ADBI).** Landlocked DMC dependency on transit countries (LAO
on VNM/THA, MNG on CHN, central Asia on RUS/CHN) is the dominant
friction story for those economies; aggregating with coastal DMCs
loses the mechanism.

**C-4 (OECD ITF).** OECD trade-cost data provides bilateral
trade-cost figures; per-corridor friction would be a more actionable
measure than the country-level aggregate.

## Responses

All accepted. Article frames LPI as a perception-of-logistics-gap
proxy. Landlocked subset analysis deferred. AIS / OECD trade-cost
upgrade-pass documented.
