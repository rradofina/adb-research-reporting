# Why a national logistics proxy failed the port-time test

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

Port and corridor diagnostics influence where governments and development
institutions investigate logistics bottlenecks. The inherited screen in this
program multiplied import scale by a national Logistics Performance Index gap
and labeled the result port-hinterland friction. Those inputs describe trade
exposure and perceived logistics conditions; neither observes vessel time or
the route from the port gate to an inland destination.

The study therefore asks a falsifiable measurement question: does that
national ordering identify the same economies as the World Bank Container Port
Performance Index, an observed vessel-time object? In the 2025 main test, only
Indonesia remains in the inherited top five. China moves from inherited rank 1
to observed-disadvantage rank 12, and India moves from 2 to 10.

The reader should remember the decision, not a replacement ranking. The
imports × LPI construct is retired as a measure of port or hinterland friction.
CPPI supports a port-boundary diagnostic, while the inland leg remains
unmeasured. Maturity: **Prepared pipeline under §18 AI-first; not externally
reviewed.**
