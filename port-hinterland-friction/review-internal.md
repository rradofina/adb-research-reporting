# Internal review — Port-Hinterland Friction

`attestation_chain: ai-first`. §18 critique-pass. Closed 2026-04-26.

## Critique

1. The index combines large absolute import-volume with mid-range LPI.
   The top-5 are large economies; this risks a "size = friction"
   tautology. Per-capita-import or import-share-of-GDP normalization
   would surface different DMCs.
2. LPI is a 2023 survey, not continuous. Annual variability is masked.
3. Landlocked DMCs (LAO, MNG, central Asia) have structurally
   different friction (transit-country dependence on neighbor's port).
   The current index treats all DMCs uniformly.

## Responses

1. Article reports both absolute and per-capita rankings; absolute
   used as the headline because that's what the friction-exposure
   measure (LPI gap × trade volume) is directly designed to test.
2. LPI vintage flagged.
3. Landlocked DMCs documented in `limitations.md`; subset analysis
   deferred to §18.5 upgrade-pass.
