# Coastal informal risk — operating status

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · Last updated: 2026-07-19

- **Finding:** The inherited national proxy does not identify a coastal
  settlement, a hazard footprint, or informality.
- **Decision:** Start from a public coastal spatial object and a rough visual;
  do not polish or extend the existing country ranking.
- **Stage:** L1 claim framing and L2 source qualification.
- **Highest-leverage move:** Pilot one low-elevation coastal settlement-growth
  object using GHSL population/built-up change and a public elevation/coast mask.
- **Reason:** It can show where exposed settlement growth is observable while
  keeping informality outside the empirical claim.
- **Stop rule:** If the settlement × elevation join cannot support a defensible
  local comparison, publish the coverage/construct limitation and rotate.
- **Nonclaim:** Remote sensing alone cannot identify informal tenure,
  infrastructure adequacy, storm-surge loss, or planning failure.
