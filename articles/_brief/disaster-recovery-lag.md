# What we found

Do not use the current country burden ranking or GDIS-centroid night-light pilot as a recovery indicator: three of five burden metrics change the claimed top two, and zero of seven Haiyan centroids have one recovery month across 54 specifications.

# What this means

What remains usable:

- EM-DAT for explicitly named burden measures.
- GDIS as a queue of approximate administrative locations.
- Light Every Night as a scalable public radiance source.
- The pilot as a diagnostic of observation coverage and parameter dependence.

A stronger design needs verified footprints, longer baselines, settlement-aware aggregation, comparisons, and an independent recovery outcome.

# What this does not say

No welfare recovery, service restoration, reconstruction, resilience ranking, or causal effect. Zero recovery-month detections under the tested specifications are a measurement result for this design, not a claim that no recovery occurred.
