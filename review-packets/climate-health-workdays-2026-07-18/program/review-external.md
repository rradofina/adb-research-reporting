# External-perspective synthesis — Climate-health labor capacity

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · §18.4 synthesis 2026-07-18

No individual reviewer was contacted. The objections below synthesize issues
that a labor economist, occupational-health specialist, exposure scientist,
national statistician, and policy reader would be expected to raise from the
cited public literature and source documentation.

## Objections and disposition

### “A PM2.5 proxy cannot be tested as though it were a heat model.”

Accepted. The paper treats the mismatch as the result and preserves the
separate PM2.5-productivity literature. It does not combine the pathways.

### “Potential work hours lost are not observed absence.”

Accepted. Every public surface uses modelled-potential language and names the
unjoined outcome gap.

### “National sector shares and exposure averages hide workplace variation.”

Accepted. The limitation is structural. A future extension requires aligned
subnational exposure, employment, adaptation, and outcome data.

### “The unit conversion must be independently visible.”

Accepted. The reproduction guide states that source sector totals are
thousands of hours; the committed script converts them and preserves hashes.

### “A league table can imply blame.”

Accepted. The primary chart visualizes disagreement between constructs. The
2024 rate and scale charts are planning descriptions with explicit non-claims.

### “The source license must travel with the data.”

Accepted. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 is recorded in provenance, versions, generated
evidence, and documentation.

## Synthesis decision

Proceed at PP with the construct-validation headline. Do not advance to an
observed-effect or policy-impact claim without a new outcome data object and
owner-led domain review.
