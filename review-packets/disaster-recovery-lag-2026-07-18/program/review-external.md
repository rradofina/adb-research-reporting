# External red-team synthesis

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

No individual external reviewer was contacted. This is an AI synthesis of the
strongest objections implied by the cited literature and source documentation.

## Strongest objections

1. **Outcome validity:** A radiance return can occur without household or
   service recovery, and recovery can occur without returning to the old light
   level.
2. **Treatment geography:** Administrative centroids and square windows mix
   affected and unaffected locations and can miss the true storm path.
3. **Baseline instability:** Sparse valid pre-event months and dark radiance
   values make ratios sensitive to small processing changes.
4. **Reference validity:** Manila shares national shocks and seasonal patterns;
   it is a normalization reference, not an untreated control.
5. **Event selection:** A single prominent storm cannot establish cross-event
   performance, especially across different cloud, settlement, and power-system
   regimes.
6. **Burden comparability:** EM-DAT human and monetary fields reflect thresholds,
   revisions, valuation, and reporting capacity as well as underlying harm.
7. **Geometry validation:** A 1,000 km screen is too coarse to establish local
   accuracy, though useful for catching gross errors.

## Response in the paper

All seven objections constrain the headline. The paper reports failure of the
measurement chain, not recovery performance. It keeps the source pipeline as a
reusable asset and names verified footprints, longer baselines, comparison
areas, and independent outcomes as requirements for the next design.

Publish the negative construct result at `PP`; do not promote to human-final or
reuse candidate months as substantive findings.
