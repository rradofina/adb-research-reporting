# The finding

| ISO3 | Water withdrawal % | Cereal yield kg/ha | Rural % | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TKM | 1,868% | (low) | (high) | transboundary (Amu Darya) |
| PAK | 326% | (mid) | (high) | within-country, Indus basin |
| AZE | 161% | (mid) | (mid) | within-country |
| AFG | (high) | (low) | 75% | within-country |

The set is **mixed in mechanism**: TKM's pressure is upstream-
cooperation-dependent (Amu Darya inflow used for irrigation), distinct
from the other three's within-country water-intensity. The article
does not aggregate the mechanisms.

# What it cannot say

- **Trajectory water stress** (current vs projected) requires WRI
  Aqueduct or CCKP precip projections; this artifact is a single
  point-in-time measure.
- **Crop-specific sensitivity** — cereal yield is a partial proxy
  for cotton-dominant (TKM, AZE) or livestock-dominant economies.
- **Subnational stress** within Pakistan (Indus basin vs Balochistan)
  is hidden by the country mean.
- **Aral Basin transboundary cooperation** (UZB, KGZ, TJK, TKM, KAZ)
  is the dominant mechanism for Central Asia and not captured by a
  per-country index.
