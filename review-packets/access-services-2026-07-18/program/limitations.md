# Limitations — Access services

`attestation_chain: ai-first`. Updated 2026-07-18.

1. **Point counts are not access.** The analysis does not model road networks,
   terrain, travel mode, border or river barriers, congestion, opening hours,
   referral pathways, or travel cost.
2. **A facility is not a unit of capacity.** OSM and registry points do not
   reveal staffing, beds, services, quality, stock-outs, operating status, or
   utilization.
3. **Taxonomies differ.** OSM tags and official clinical-facility categories
   are not guaranteed to describe the same provider universe.
4. **The Cambodia comparison is asynchronous.** It compares a 2010 public
   inventory with a 2026 OSM snapshot. Phnom Penh has 227 OSM health points
   versus 22 facilities in the older public inventory; this cannot be read as
   overmapping or completeness without a current crosswalk.
5. **Only two pilot economies have comparable registry corrections.** A common
   corrected eight-economy rank is not available.
6. **Population and boundary vintages vary.** ADM1 names and boundaries can
   change; Tbong Khmum is unmatched in the 2010 Cambodia source because the
   province was created after that source vintage.
7. **Associations are descriptive.** The Philippine completeness relationship
   does not identify causality and may also reflect taxonomy or geocoding
   differences.
8. **The pilot is not ADB-wide.** Eight economies and 104 ADM1 units do not
   support inference to all ADB DMCs.
9. **No performance or welfare rank is produced.** The former composite is a
   triage device only and is not the headline.
10. **Review is AI-first.** No named external expert was contacted. Human-final
    use requires the owner-led and reviewer steps in `upgrade-gap.md`.
