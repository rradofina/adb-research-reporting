# Internal review — Water Stress × Crop Diversification

`attestation_chain: ai-first`. §18 critique-pass. Closed 2026-04-26.

## Critique

1. TKM's 1,868% withdrawal-as-share-of-resources is structurally
   different — it reflects Amu Darya transboundary inflow used for
   irrigation. Treating this as "stress" is correct but the
   mechanism is upstream-cooperation-dependent, not just within-
   country.
2. Cereal yield kg/ha may be a poor proxy for water-stress sensitivity
   in cotton-dominant or livestock-dominant economies (TKM, AZE,
   parts of PAK).
3. Rural-population multiplier loads the index toward agricultural
   economies regardless of actual water stress; this is the intended
   compounding but should be transparent.

## Responses

1. Article frames TKM as transboundary-dependent, distinguished from
   AFG/PAK/AZE.
2. Crop-specific water-sensitivity deferred to §18.5 upgrade-pass.
3. Rural-multiplier rationale documented.
