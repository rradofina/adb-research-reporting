# Decision in one line

Do not use the current country burden ranking or GDIS-centroid night-light
pilot as a recovery indicator: three of five burden metrics change the claimed
top two, and zero of seven Haiyan centroids have one recovery month across 54
specifications.

## What is usable

- EM-DAT for explicitly named burden measures.
- GDIS as a queue of approximate administrative locations.
- Light Every Night as a scalable public radiance source.
- The pilot as a diagnostic of observation coverage and parameter dependence.

## What is not established

No welfare recovery, service restoration, reconstruction, resilience ranking,
or causal effect. A stronger design needs verified footprints, longer
baselines, settlement-aware aggregation, comparisons, and an independent
recovery outcome.
