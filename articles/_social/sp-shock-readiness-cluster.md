# Short post

> A stable formula can still rank the wrong records. Only 3 of 5 economies in
> our shock-payment “top five” survive the panel's own value order. Every named
> economy had a documented COVID cash-transfer response—but 0 comparable
> delivery outcomes are joined.

# Expanded post

> The original screen looked robust under ±50% weight changes. Then we checked
> the missing-data rule.
>
> Vanuatu and Tajikistan have higher computed values than the Philippines and
> Bangladesh, but were omitted because each lacked one proxy leg. A narrower
> safety-net variant shares zero members with the published set, though its old
> vintages prevent a replacement ranking.
>
> The result is a construct failure, not a new country league table. The next
> study needs actual receipt, failure, and delivery-time data.
