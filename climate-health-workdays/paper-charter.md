# Paper charter — Climate-health labor capacity

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

Last updated: 2026-07-18.

Heat can cut what workers can physiologically do on a given day. A convenient
national screen that multiplies outdoor air pollution by outdoor-employment
share is easy to build from public tables—but climate-health analysts, labor
teams, and statistics offices need to know whether that screen recovers the
same economies as a purpose-built heat–labor measure before treating it as a
heat-and-work signal.

## One research question

Does the inherited national PM2.5 × outdoor-employment proxy recover the
cross-economy ordering of the Lancet Countdown 2025 indicator 1.1.3 estimate
of heat-related potential work hours lost?

## Contribution

The paper turns an internally stable proxy rank into an external construct
test. It aligns annual WDI and Lancet data, tests the full ranking and top-three
set, repairs a total-population worker denominator, and separates modelled
potential capacity loss from observed labor outcomes.

## Primary claim

Across all 21 aligned year-and-parameter tests, the proxy shares at most one of
its top three economies with the heat-related potential work-hours-loss
measure. Sixteen tests have zero overlap; five have one.

## Decision rule

Reject the proxy as a substitute for the heat construct if the baseline top
threes are usually disjoint and no ±50% parameter test produces more than one
shared economy. Report full-rank Spearman correlation as a diagnostic, not as
the sole decision statistic.

## Non-claims

- No observed absenteeism, hours-worked, output, wage, or welfare estimate.
- No causal effect estimated by this study.
- No claim that PM2.5 has no productivity effect.
- No heat-plus-pollution composite.
- No economy performance or policy-quality ranking.
- No authoritative all-DMC roster claim.

## Stopping rule

This issue is complete when the unit-correct evidence, 21-test comparison,
figures, paper, publication ladder, review packet, public route, and boards
agree. Reopening requires a new observed labor-outcome object or a pre-specified
subnational exposure-outcome join—not another national proxy pass.
