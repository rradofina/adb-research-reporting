# Internal review — Invisible Urbanization

`attestation_chain: ai-first`. §18 critique-pass. Closed 2026-04-26.

## Critique

1. The index is "rural-share × urban-growth." It surfaces low-urban-
   base + high-urban-growth DMCs, but doesn't directly measure
   "invisible" urbanization (settlement growth not yet reflected in
   urban classification).
2. WDI urban classification is country-defined; what counts as "urban"
   in PNG vs BGD is not comparable.
3. GHSL Built-up Surface (JRC) is the canonical satellite-based
   measure. Without it, the "invisible" framing is aspirational.

## Responses

1. The signal is honest as a *proxy*; the framing in the article
   is precise: "growth from a low-urban base, suggesting sub-
   classification lag."
2. WDI urban-definition heterogeneity flagged in limitations.md.
3. GHSL integration is the §18.5 upgrade-pass.
