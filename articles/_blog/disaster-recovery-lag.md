It is easy to mistake a successful data pipeline for a successful research
measure. We downloaded 108 fixed daily night-light observations, linked them to
seven Typhoon Haiyan administrative centroids, applied quality filters, and
tested 54 recovery definitions. The infrastructure worked.

The result did not stabilize. No centroid returned one recovery month across
all variants, and only one had four valid pre-event baseline months. That is
the useful finding: public data access is scalable, but the proposed construct
needs better event footprints, baselines, and independent outcomes before it
can support a recovery claim.
