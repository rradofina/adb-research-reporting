# The finding

| ISO3 | Top fuel | Share | Subtype |
|---|---|---|---|
| BTN | Hydro | 100% | hydro-dependent |
| BRN | Gas | 100% | gas-dependent |
| NPL | Hydro | 95% | hydro-dependent |
| MNG | Coal | 89% | coal-dependent |
| TJK | Hydro | 88% | hydro-dependent |

Stable across baseline fuel-Herfindahl ranking and the alternative
single-fuel-share-≥-80% threshold.

# Subtypes mean different fragility kinds

- **Hydro-dependent (BTN, NPL, TJK).** Renewable, but exposed to
  drought-driven generation reduction. The fragility mechanism is
  inter-annual precipitation variability and snowmelt timing.
- **Gas-dependent (BRN).** Fossil-fuel single-source, exposed to
  global gas price swings and (long-term) carbon-transition risk.
- **Coal-dependent (MNG).** Fossil-fuel single-source, exposed to
  carbon-transition risk and heat-stressed combustion efficiency.

The article does not aggregate these. They live in different
fragility families.

# What it cannot say

- WRI v1.3.0 frozen 2022; rapid solar buildouts in IND, VNM, and
  others are missing. The single-fuel ranking for the top-5 is
  unaffected (their dominance was 80%+ in 2022 and is unlikely to
  flip in three years), but ADB DMC diversification figures
  generally are stale.
- Capacity-share Herfindahl ≠ generation-share. Plants run at
  different load factors; generation-share (data not in WRI) would
  shift figures.
- Heat-derated reliability (the program name's question) requires
  temperature-derated generation curves, not in this artifact.
