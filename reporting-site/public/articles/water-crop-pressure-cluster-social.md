# Short post

> A stable formula can still measure the wrong things. Our inherited
> water-crop “top four” is the actual raw top four in only 2 of 7 runs. Direct
> available-water stress retains 2 of 4 published members. Direct crop
> concentration retains 0 of 4.

# Expanded post

> The old screen divided withdrawals by internal renewable water, used inverse
> cereal yield as “crop pressure,” and multiplied by rural population.
>
> Replacing those proxies changes the evidence. FAOSTAT covers 41 roster
> economies, but water stress covers 30—and all five highest crop-HHI records
> lack water data. Among aligned rows, the replacement diagnostic correlates
> +0.92 with water stress and only +0.05 with crop concentration.
>
> The result is a construct and observability failure, not a new country league
> table. The next object must join water, crops, irrigation, and exposure at
> basin × crop × year level.
