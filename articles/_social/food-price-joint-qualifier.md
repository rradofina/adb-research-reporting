# Short post

> Correcting the price outcome leaves 152 coarse-rice spike cells across 12
> Nepal markets. Only 17 follow locally dry rainfall at a one-month lag. Dry
> alignment remains a minority in all 81 ±50% threshold runs. This is a
> construct-validation finding—not a climate-effect estimate.

# Expanded post

> The earlier method compared prices with a full-sample calendar-month median,
> mechanically classifying later high levels as anomalies. Year-on-year change
> cuts flagged cells from 259 to 152 and broad wave months from 25 to 10.
>
> A broad 2023–2024 wave remains visible, but local dryness rarely aligns with
> it. The annual CPI/imports ranking also misses Nepal and cannot observe a
> commodity, event, or market mechanism.
>
> The next study needs recorded geocoded hazards, multiple commodities, crop
> and sourcing zones, roads, fuel, exchange rates, trade, policy controls, and
> a pre-specified causal design.
