Country disaster burden is not recovery.

We tested the distinction twice. Three of five burden metrics changed the
claimed top two. Then a 108-orbit Typhoon Haiyan night-light pilot produced no
centroid with one recovery month across 54 specifications.

The public archive is scalable. The recovery construct is not yet validated.
That boundary is the finding—not a reason to hide the negative result.
