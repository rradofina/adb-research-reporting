   a stable school calendar and enrollment denominator?
2. Can daily wet-bulb globe temperature or humid-heat exposure be joined to
   school locations without confusing modeled outdoor heat with indoor heat?
3. Which adaptation variables—cooling, ventilation, building material,
   schedule shifts, remote learning—are observable before outcome analysis?
4. Are closure, attendance, assessment, or learning outcomes the most
   traceable first endpoint in a public-data pilot?
5. How should repeated disruptions and students affected by multiple events be
   counted?
6. What missingness process determines whether an event enters public
   international reporting?
7. Which one-country or one-city pilot has enough event variation to test a
   nonlinear heat response without constructing another national composite?
