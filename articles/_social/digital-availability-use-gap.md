# Primary post

Reported 4G availability exceeded internet use by a median **14.3 percentage
points** across 34 ADB developing member economies with exact-year ITU data in
2024. The difference was positive in 31.

Coverage means living within signal range. Use means having used the Internet
in the previous three months. They are not interchangeable—and their
difference is not a person-level count.

The practical lesson: show availability and use together, then add quality,
affordability, devices, and skills before diagnosing why people remain offline.

Source: ITU DataHub. AI-first prepared research paper; public data and
reproduction scripts included.

# Short version

Network rollout is not internet adoption.

2024 exact-year ITU data show a 14.3-point median gap between reported 4G
availability and internet use across 34 observed ADB developing member cases.

Measure both. Keep the denominator. Do not let speed-test users stand in for
the unconnected.
