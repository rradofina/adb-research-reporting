# Internal review — Flood Market Access

`attestation_chain: ai-first`. §18 critique-pass. Closed 2026-04-26.

## Critique

1. The "market access" element is rural-share × flood-frequency. This
   doesn't measure access — it measures rural exposure. Article must
   be precise.
2. EM-DAT thresholds (≥10 deaths or ≥100 affected) under-report small
   floods that disrupt rural markets but don't cross thresholds.
3. Top-4 reflects population × frequency. Per-capita affected rate
   would surface different DMCs (Pacific micro-states with large
   population shares affected by single events).

## Responses

1. Article frames as "flood-driven rural exposure" rather than
   "market access" specifically. Market-access integration deferred.
2. EM-DAT under-reporting flagged.
3. Per-capita variant deferred to §18.5 upgrade-pass.
