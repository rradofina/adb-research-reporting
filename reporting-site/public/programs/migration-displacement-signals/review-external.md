## Candidate perspectives and objections

### UN DESA Population Division — stock construction

**Basis:** International Migrant Stock 2024 and *International Migration and
Sustainable Development* [@undesa2024migrant; @undesa2024sustainablemigration].

**Synthesized objection.** The paper must distinguish stock by origin from
annual migration flow. The 2024 release combines newly reassessed series with
extrapolations from the prior vintage, so a cross-economy rank is not a common
quality grade.

**Response.** Accepted. The paper uses stock language throughout, reports the
release's mixed update basis in the literature and limitations, and makes no
flow or source-quality ranking.

### World Bank migration-data perspective — bilateral comparability

**Basis:** bilateral stock harmonization and reliability literature
[@ozden2011where].

**Synthesized objection.** A bilateral stock matrix is assembled from national
censuses and registers with different definitions and adjustment needs.
Ranking changes may reflect both true demographic structure and measurement
systems.

**Response.** Accepted. The result is framed as a denominator and construct
audit. It does not attribute rank movement to a single causal mechanism.

### Migration-theory perspective — aspirations and capabilities

**Basis:** aspirations-capabilities framework and welfare literature
[@dehaas2021aspirations; @clemens2011economics].

**Synthesized objection.** A high stock share is not a direct measure of
pressure, deprivation, desire to migrate, or welfare loss. People can have
high aspirations but low capability to move, or substantial mobility through
opportunity and established networks.

**Response.** Accepted. The paper bans pressure, deficiency, and propensity
interpretations and ends with source-selection uses rather than normative
rankings.

### ADB Pacific labor-market perspective — small-economy interpretation

**Basis:** Pacific labor-market and mobility evidence
[@adb2018pacificlabour; @anu2023palm].

**Synthesized objection.** Samoa and Tonga combine older diaspora networks,
formal labor pathways, circulation, and small resident populations. Their
shares cannot be read as current outflow rates or as directly comparable with
large origin economies.

**Response.** Accepted. The population-share chart labels cumulative stock,
and the paper identifies a future flow and migration-purpose object as the
necessary upgrade.

### UNHCR and IDMC — category boundaries

**Basis:** UNHCR population-statistics methodology and IDMC internal-
displacement reporting [@unhcr2024methodology; @idmc2024grid].

**Synthesized objection.** Refugees, asylum-seekers, other people in need of
international protection, and IDPs are distinct statistical groups. UNHCR
stock cannot partition all migrant stock, and IDPs cannot be inserted into an
international emigrant denominator.

**Response.** Accepted. The script sums only the named international forced-
displacement categories, excludes IDPs from the comparison, and labels the
remaining stock unclassified by purpose.
