# Paper charter — Migration and displacement signals

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

Last updated: 2026-07-18.

## Decision audience

Migration analysts, Pacific operations teams, national statistics offices,
and development practitioners deciding which origin economies require a
stock, flow, or forced-displacement evidence lens.

## One research question

How much does the identity of the leading emigrant-origin economies change
when UN DESA 2024 emigrant stock is divided by WDI 2024 origin population,
and does the UNHCR forced-displacement crosswalk change the interpretation of
the population-share leaders?

## Contribution

The paper turns a familiar absolute diaspora-size ranking into a denominator
and construct audit for the 44-economy program panel. It reports the rank
inversion, makes the three missing population denominators visible, and uses
UNHCR only to identify the forced-displacement component that the broader UN
DESA stock cannot distinguish.

## Primary claim

The absolute and population-share top fives have zero overlap. Samoa, Tonga,
Armenia, Nauru, and Fiji replace India, China, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, and
the Philippines. Afghanistan is the near-rank exception at share rank 6, but
UNHCR forced-displacement stock equals 81.7% of its UN DESA emigrant stock;
none of the population-share top five is forced-displacement-majority.

## Decision rule

Reshape the absolute-stock headline when the absolute and population-share
top-five sets overlap by at most 50%. Test the arbitrary overlap threshold at
25%, 50%, and 75%, and test the top-N choice at 3, 5, and 8. Classify a row as
forced-displacement-majority at 50%, with 25% and 75% sensitivity checks.

## Non-claims

- No current migration-flow or departure-rate estimate.
- No migration-propensity, welfare, vulnerability, or causal claim.
- No classification of labor, family, student, or temporary-work migration.
- No internal-displacement estimate.
- No claim that a larger population share is better or worse.

## Stopping rule

This issue is complete when the denominator switch, forced-displacement
exception, missing denominators, sensitivity suite, literature framing,
figures, paper, publication ladder, review packet, and public route agree.
Further progress requires a new flow or migration-purpose data object and is
a separate issue, not another pass at the stock ranking.
