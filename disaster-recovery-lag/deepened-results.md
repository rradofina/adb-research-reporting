# Deepened results — from burden screen to construct test

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

## Decision

The program no longer presents a country disaster-burden pair as a
recovery-lag result. The original pair fails its registered metric-switch rule,
and the direct nighttime-light pilot fails its frozen construct rule.

## Stage 1: metric falsification

`generated/disaster-recovery-lag-metric-falsification.json` recomputes five
burden views. The inherited CHN–IND pair appears under total affected and
adjusted damage only. Events per year selects CHN–IDN; deaths selects IDN–MMR;
the indicative per-million view selects TUV–MHL. The overall kill condition
fires.

## Stage 2: source readiness

`generated/disaster-recovery-lag-recovery-source-readiness.json` identifies a
2012–2018 GDIS–VIIRS overlap with 609 unique GDIS IDs, 565 disaster numbers,
and 2,881 location rows in ADB developing member economies. This converts an
abstract source wish into a concrete event queue, but does not create a
recovery outcome.

## Stage 3: direct extraction

`generated/disaster-recovery-haiyan-construct-validation.json` records the
Typhoon Haiyan case design and results. Seven GDIS centroids are evaluated over
18 months using 108 fixed scheduled orbits. The main rule finds candidate
months for Aklan, Palawan, and Samar, but only Samar reaches four valid
baseline months.

The full 54-variant matrix returns zero stable centroids. This is the claim-
changing result. The correct interpretation is that the construct is not yet
validated—not that no place recovered.

## Stage 4: geometry screen

`generated/disaster-recovery-gdis-geometry-audit.json` flags 3 of 2,881
candidate centroids more than 1,000 km from the associated Natural Earth
country polygon. The screen identifies gross pipeline risk while preserving
the larger limitation: administrative centroids are not impact footprints.

## Progress test

The claim changed shape. A reader is better off because the package now
separates burden, data access, measurement validity, and welfare interpretation
instead of compressing them into a recovery ranking.
