# Retired result — the WDI multiplier ranking

`attestation_chain: ai-first`

The former program ranked economies using:

`rural share × positive urban-population growth × 10`

and labelled the leaders “invisible urbanization.” That result is retired.

## Why it failed

- It contains no built-up surface, settlement geometry, administrative
  classification, or classification history.
- Multiplying every row by 0.5, 1.0, or 1.5 cannot change a rank by
  construction, so the reported ±50% stability was tautological.
- Fast urban-population growth from a rural base does not demonstrate that
  settlement change is absent from official classifications.
- It encouraged a country ranking where the real research object was a
  disagreement between definitions.

## Replacement

The new program uses the official GHS-DUC administrative tables and WDI urban
share series. It reports construct disagreement, scale sensitivity, and unit
transitions with explicit nonclaims. The old top five—PNG, SLB, AFG, LAO, and
BGD—is not carried forward as a finding.
