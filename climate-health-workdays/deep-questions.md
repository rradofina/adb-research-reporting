# Deep questions — Climate-health labor capacity

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · 2026-07-18

## Questions answered in this issue

1. **Does the old proxy recover the direct heat construct?** No. Maximum
   top-three overlap is one across 21 tests.
2. **Is disagreement confined to a cutoff?** No. Full-rank correlations are
   0.12–0.17.
3. **Does ±50% proxy tuning restore agreement?** No.
4. **Did the old worker count use the right denominator?** No. Total population
   materially overstated the selected counts.
5. **Does the heat source cover the roster?** It covers 43/44 rows in 2024;
   the companion outdoor-worker source covers 42/44.
6. **Does this package observe actual time off work?** No.

## Questions deliberately left open

1. Do modelled potential hours predict observed absenteeism, hours, or output
   within workplaces or subnational regions?
2. How much do cooling, shade, shift timing, rest, mechanization, and worker
   acclimatization change the exposure-outcome relationship?
3. Are heat and PM2.5 additive, interactive, or separable in a design with both
   exposures and a common labor outcome?
4. How different are national and subnational rankings when exposure,
   employment, and outcomes are aligned at the same geography and vintage?
5. Which public administrative or survey sources can provide reproducible
   labor outcomes without confidentiality or licensing conflicts?
