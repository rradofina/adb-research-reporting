# The finding

| ISO3 | DMC | Rural % | Flood events 2000-2025 | Index |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IND | India | high | many | 48.6 |
| IDN | Indonesia | mid | many | 30.0 |
| CHN | China | mid | many | 28.1 |
| AFG | Afghanistan | high | several | 20.9 |

Stable across full-index baseline, flood-events-only, and rural ×
floods alternatives. Top-5 is metric-sensitive (Pakistan and
Philippines drop in flood-events-only; Bangladesh and Nepal drop in
multiplicative variants); honest narrowing to top-4.

# What it cannot say

- "Market access" requires road-network and all-weather-road data,
  not in this artifact. The current proxy is rural-share × flood-
  frequency.
- EM-DAT thresholds undercount small recurrent floods.
- Modeled flood extent (GLOFAS, FATHOM) is the upgrade-pass for
  forward-looking exposure.
- Per-capita affected populations are absorbed by the absolute-
  frequency metric. Pacific micro-states with large per-capita
  flood impacts don't surface in the top-4.
- Sub-national disaggregation (within-IND, within-CHN, within-IDN)
  hidden by the country-level aggregation.
