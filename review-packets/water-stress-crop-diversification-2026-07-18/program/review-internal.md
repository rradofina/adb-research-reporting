# Internal review — Water stress × crop diversification

`attestation_chain: ai-first` · §18 critique pass · 2026-07-18

## Critique 1 — the direct crop-HHI leaders may be small-system artifacts

Tuvalu, Kiribati, the Federated States of Micronesia, Nauru, and Vanuatu have
few reported crop items and very high coconut shares. Their HHI values may be
sensitive to reporting completeness, aggregate-item filtering, and the small
absolute area involved. Calling them the “most vulnerable” would overstate the
source.

**Response.** Accepted. The paper calls them the highest observed national
harvested-area concentration records, not the most vulnerable economies. It
shows top crop shares, reports missing water rows, and makes no policy ranking.

## Critique 2 — top-four versus top-five comparisons are asymmetric

The inherited claim names four economies while direct constructs are compared
against top fives. A top-five comparison is more permissive and could make the
old set look better.

**Response.** Accepted and intentional. The direct tests ask whether published
members appear even within a wider five-economy window. Water retains only two
and crop HHI none, so a stricter top-four comparison would not rescue the
claim.

## Critique 3 — the correlation is selected on observed water rows

The 30 aligned economies exclude 11 crop-visible cases, including all five
crop-HHI leaders. The -0.24 correlation cannot describe the roster.

**Response.** Accepted. The coverage funnel and scatter expose the omitted
cases. The result is explicitly conditional on the aligned sample, and the
selection itself is a central observability finding.

## Critique 4 — the replacement composite repeats the original mistake

Multiplying water, HHI, and rural share could create another arbitrary country
ranking.

**Response.** Accepted. The score is retained only as a diagnostic for driver
dominance, ablation, and ±50% sensitivity. It is never a headline or policy
ranking. Its water dominance is evidence against promoting it.

## Critique 5 — transboundary interpretation can outrun the indicator

The difference between internal-resource and available-water ratios does not by
itself identify Amu Darya or Indus mechanisms, depletion, reuse, or treaty
security.

**Response.** Accepted. The paper describes transboundary reliance as context
supported by literature and says the national series cannot separate the
mechanism. It does not label values above 100% as over-pumping.

## Review decision

Pass for PP construct-validation publication. Reject promotion to a water-crop
exposure or resilience finding until the basin × crop × irrigation × year join
exists and an observed outcome is tested.
