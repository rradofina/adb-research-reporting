# The finding

Five DMCs hold the top of the readiness-gap ranking:

| ISO3 | Poverty headcount % | Payment-system readiness | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAK | 23 | 22 (SP) + 21 (account) | high |
| MMR | 10 | 14 (SP) + low (account) | high |
| LAO | (high) | 2 (SP) + low (account) | high |
| BGD | (high) | (computed) | high |
| PHL | (high) | (computed) | high |

Stable across SP/account weighting perturbations.

# What it cannot say

- **ASPIRE includes non-shock-responsive programs.** Pensions don't
  scale during crisis. The gap measure is generic, not specifically
  shock-response.
- **Findex 2021 was elevated** by pandemic emergency programs.
  Account ownership figures are a high-water mark; a 2025 vintage
  will likely show different baselines.
- **Account ownership ≠ delivery capability.** Many low-income
  households have dormant accounts.
- **Subnational variation is large.** Pakistan's BISP penetrates
  Sindh and Punjab strongly while leaving Balochistan thin; the
  national figure obscures.
