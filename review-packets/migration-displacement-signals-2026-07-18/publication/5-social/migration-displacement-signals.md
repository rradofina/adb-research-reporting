# Short post

> A migration ranking can reverse when the denominator changes. The five
> largest origin stocks—led by India and China—have zero overlap with the five
> highest stocks relative to resident population—led by Samoa and Tonga.
> Stock is not flow or propensity.

# Expanded post

> What question does a migrant-stock ranking answer? In a 44-economy panel,
> the absolute top five are India, China, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, and the
> Philippines. Among 41 economies with WDI population data, the population-
> share top five are Samoa, Tonga, Armenia, Nauru, and Fiji: zero overlap.
>
> Afghanistan is the separate construct warning. It ranks sixth on the
> population-share measure, while UNHCR forced-displacement stock equals 81.7%
> of its UN DESA emigrant stock. None of the population-share top five reaches
> a 25% forced-displacement share.
>
> The evidence supports separate readings of diaspora scale, stock relative to
> resident population, and forced displacement. It does not estimate current
> flows, migration propensity, labor-migration purpose, welfare, or causes.
